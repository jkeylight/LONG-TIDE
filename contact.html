Getting here

Buses stop three minutes away and there are two paid car parks within a five-minute walk. The room is on the first floor, up one flight of stairs — tell me if you need step-free access and I will arrange an alternative.

If this is urgent, please don’t wait for me.

Craniosacral therapy is for persistent, ongoing difficulties. If you have sudden severe pain, a head injury, anything affecting speech or vision, or a newborn with a fever, contact emergency or urgent medical services first. Then come and see me afterwards.
