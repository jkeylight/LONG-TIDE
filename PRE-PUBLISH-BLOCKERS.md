
Two notes on wording, whenever that happens:

- **Before completion**, the only truthful framing is status disclosure — "in
  training", "student", "completing". The words *practitioner*, *qualified*,
  *registered*, *diploma* and any postnominal (RCST, BCST) should not appear
  until they apply.
- **"Est. 2019"** should become either the true first year of practice or be
  deleted; a founding date that predates the training cannot stand.
