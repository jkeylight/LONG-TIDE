Legal · 01

Privacy notice

Version 1.0Applies from 16 November 2026India · DPDP Act 2023

The short version. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from any other company. The only information I receive is what you choose to type into the enquiry form, and I use it to answer you. Nothing else is collected, sold or shared.

01 · Who is responsible

This website is operated by Poonam Mahendra, a craniosacral therapy practice trading as Long Tide, based in Mumbai, India. For the purposes of data protection law I am the controller of any personal information submitted through it.

Data protection enquiries: poonam@craniosacraltherapy.com.

02 · What this website does not do

These statements are verifiable from the page source, and are the reason this notice is short:

  • No cookies. None are set, first-party or otherwise.
  • No analytics or tracking. There is no Google Analytics, no pixel, no session recording.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts, scripts and images are all served from this site. Nothing is loaded from a CDN, an advertising network or a social platform, so no other company can see that you visited.
  • No social media buttons. Those embed trackers even when nobody clicks them.
  • No advertising.

One technical exception, which holds no personal information: while you move between pages this site writes a single value to your browser's sessionStorage so the page-transition animation knows to play. It contains no identifier, and it is erased the moment it is read — typically within a second.

03 · What I collect, and why

The only collection point is the enquiry form on the contact page. If you use it I receive:

  • your name and email address (required);
  • your phone number, if you choose to give it;
  • the general reason you selected from a list;
  • anything you write in the free-text box.

I use it for one purpose: to reply to your enquiry and, if you go on to book, to arrange and administer your appointments. I do not use it for marketing, and I will not add you to a mailing list.

04 · Health information

The free-text box is free text, so you may choose to describe symptoms, a diagnosis or an injury. In law that is sensitive personal data, and it deserves to be handled carefully.

The form cannot be submitted without ticking the consent box, which records that you agree to your enquiry — including anything health-related in it — being used to reply to you. You do not have to describe anything clinical to make an enquiry. A sentence will do.

Please do not send medical records, scans, reports or identity documents through this form. If I need them, I will ask and we will arrange something appropriate.

05 · The lawful basis

For visitors in India, this processing rests on your consent under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, given by ticking the box, and on the purpose of responding to your request.

For visitors in the UK or EU, the basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR/GDPR), and for any health information, your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)).

You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to me. Withdrawal does not affect anything done before it, and it does not oblige me to delete records I am required by law or professional obligation to keep.

06 · How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not become appointments are deleted within twelve months of the last contact. If you become a client, your records are kept for seven years from your last appointment, which is the period ordinarily expected for therapy records and their insurers, and then securely destroyed.

You can ask me to delete an enquiry sooner, and I will unless I have a legal reason to keep it.

07 · Who sees it

Nobody else, with these narrow exceptions:

  • My email provider transmits and stores the message. Email is not perfectly secure, and I would rather you knew that than assumed otherwise.
  • The web host stores the site. Like any web server it may keep short-lived access logs containing IP addresses; those are not used to identify anyone.
  • Where the law requires it, or where there is a serious risk to life.

I do not sell data, and I do not share it for advertising.

08 · Your rights

You may ask to:

  • see what I hold about you, and receive a copy;
  • correct anything inaccurate;
  • have it erased, where the law allows;
  • withdraw consent, or object to how it is used;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf, in the event of death or incapacity.

Write to poonam@craniosacraltherapy.com and I will respond within thirty days. If you are in India and are not satisfied with my response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. If you are in the UK or EU, you may complain to your national supervisory authority.

09 · Children

This site is not directed at children. Where a client is under eighteen, an enquiry should be made by a parent or guardian, and their consent covers any information submitted. Under the DPDP Act, processing a child's data without verifiable parental consent is not permitted, and I will ask for it.

10 · Security

Access to enquiries is limited to me, on devices protected by a passcode and full-disk encryption. No system is perfectly secure, and email in particular travels unencrypted. If you would prefer not to use the form, telephone on +91 99878 94488 instead.

11 · Changes to this notice

If this notice changes materially I will update the version and date at the top of this page. It is worth re-reading if you return after some months.
